Advent has begun, and for creators it is one of the most natural seasons of the year to share faith.

Many churches mark the four weeks of Advent with hope, peace, joy and love. That gives any creator a simple four-week plan:
- Hope: what are you trusting God for this season?
- Peace: a tip for a calmer, less rushed December
- Joy: a testimony or a song that lifts you
- Love: a simple act of kindness or generosity

And it is not only for worship leaders. A finance creator can talk about planning Christmas spending with wisdom. A health creator can talk about rest in a busy season. A family creator can share Advent traditions at home.

Worsship is a creator platform built for the faith community, where every part of life can be explored through a Christian lens.

Which of the four themes would you build a video around?
