Christmas week is a natural time to look back at what God has done.

A testimony doesn't need to be long or polished to help someone. A simple structure works:
1. Where you were: set the scene in one or two lines
2. What God did: keep it honest and specific
3. Where you are now: what changed for you
4. A scripture to hold on to

On Worsship, creators can add scripture references to every video, so a short testimony can point people straight to the Word that carried you.

From all of us at Worsship: a very merry Christmas to you and your family.
